Worth

Offline FD, MF & net worth.

Coming soon to the App Store

Worth is a net worth tracker that treats your money as nobody’s business but yours. No sign-up, no bank linking, no servers, no analytics. The app makes zero network calls — your accounts, balances, and history live in a private database on your device, synced only through your own iCloud.

Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • One calm number: your net worth, with a monthly delta and a 12-month trend — no daily flicker, no red-green anxiety
  • A five-minute monthly ritual: walk your accounts, type the new values, see what changed
  • Built for Indian portfolios — FDs, EPF, PPF, NPS, SGB, gold in grams, real estate — alongside global stocks, crypto, and any currency
  • Fixed deposits track themselves: enter principal, rate, and dates once, and Worth computes today’s value, maturity value, and auto-renewal
  • Scan instead of typing: photograph an FD page, a holdings screenshot, or a PDF statement and on-device OCR pre-fills the entry for your review
  • Charts and projections — allocation, trends, and “at this pace you reach ₹X by year Y”
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • CSV export and import, so your data is portable, always
  • Face ID lock, Indian numbering (₹12,34,567), and a dark mode designed first

Pricing

Free: up to 3 accounts, the full monthly check-in, the dashboard, a 1-year chart, and CSV export. One purchase unlocks everything forever — unlimited accounts, all chart ranges, projections and goals, widgets, themes, and CSV import. No subscription, ever. Face ID lock and CSV export stay free for everyone: a privacy app that holds your own data hostage would be no privacy app at all.
