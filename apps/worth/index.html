Worth

iOS · No bank linking

Your net worth, without handing anyone your bank login.

Worth treats your money as nobody’s business but yours. No sign-up, no bank linking, no servers, no analytics. The app makes zero network calls — your accounts, balances and history live in a private database on your device, synced only through your own iCloud.

Features

Manual on purpose.

No bank linking
No aggregator, no read-only credentials sitting in someone else’s database. You type the numbers, and they stay yours.
Built for Indian portfolios
FDs, EPF, PPF, NPS, SGB, gold in grams and real estate — alongside global stocks, crypto and any currency.
Deposits track themselves
Enter principal, rate and dates once, and Worth computes today’s value, maturity value and auto-renewal.
Scan instead of typing
Photograph an FD page, a holdings screenshot or a PDF statement, and on-device OCR pre-fills the entry for your review.
One calm number
Your net worth with a monthly delta and a 12-month trend. No daily flicker, no red-green anxiety.
Yours to take
CSV export and import, Face ID lock, Indian numbering (₹12,34,567), and a dark mode designed first.

On device

The safest place for your finances is your own phone.

Worth has no servers of mine. Balances, account names and history live in a private database on your device and sync only through your own iCloud. There is no sign-up, no analytics and no third-party SDK reading over your shoulder.

Pricing

One purchase, no subscription.

Free covers up to 3 accounts, the full monthly check-in, the dashboard, a 1-year chart, and CSV export.

One purchase unlocks everything forever — unlimited accounts, all chart ranges, projections and goals, widgets, themes, and CSV import. No subscription, ever.

Face ID lock and CSV export stay free for everyone: a privacy app that holds your own data hostage would be no privacy app at all.
