Panchang

iOS · Free with lifetime unlock

Today’s panchang, computed for exactly where you are.

Open it and today is simply there: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and vara with precise end times, sunrise through moonset, Rahu Kaal and Abhijit Muhurat. No ads, no accounts, no network.

A look inside.

From the App Store

Auspicious and inauspicious windows, timed to your location.

Choghadiya and Hora, computed for your place and date.

Amanta or Purnimanta, one tap apart, with named Ekadashis.

Festivals and vrats for your selected place, three months out.

Features

Computed, not downloaded.

Arcminute accuracy
A professional-grade astronomical model for Sun and Moon positions with Lahiri ayanamsa, running entirely on the phone.
Works anywhere
Zero network requests. Identical behaviour on a flight, in a village, or on a mountain.
Your exact location
Current location or any of 10,000+ cities, so an Ekadashi in New Jersey lands on the correct local day — not Delhi’s.
Both month conventions
Amanta and Purnimanta, one tap apart, with named Ekadashis and Adhika months handled properly.
Festivals timed right
Diwali at pradosha, Shivaratri at nishita. The moment, not just the date.
Four languages
English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు and தமிழ், with optional Devanagari numerals and regional calendars.

On device

It never asks you who you are.

There is no account, no analytics SDK and no server to talk to. Location stays on the device and is used only to place the Sun and Moon. Free for the daily panchang; one lifetime purchase unlocks any month, any year, and every muhurta.
