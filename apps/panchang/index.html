Panchang

Your daily Hindu calendar, always at hand.

Panchang brings the traditional Hindu almanac to your iPhone. See today's tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset times, and auspicious muhurat timings — all in a clean, easy-to-read layout.

Download on the App Store Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • Daily panchang: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, and karana
  • Sunrise, sunset, moonrise, and moonset times
  • Auspicious and inauspicious timings for the day
  • Simple, distraction-free design
