Panchang

Tithi, Nakshatra & Muhurat.

Today's tithi, nakshatra and muhurat — computed entirely on your device, for your exact location. No ads, no accounts, no internet required. Open the app and today is simply there: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and vara with precise end times; sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset; Rahu Kaal and Abhijit Muhurat. Nothing else competing for your attention.

Download on the App Store Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • Computed, not downloaded — a professional-grade astronomical model (Sun and Moon to about an arcminute, Lahiri ayanamsa) running entirely on device
  • Zero network requests, so it works identically on a flight, in a village, or on a mountain
  • Your current location or any of 10,000+ cities worldwide — so an Ekadashi in New Jersey lands on the correct local day, not Delhi's
  • Amanta and Purnimanta month conventions, one tap apart, with named Ekadashis and Adhika months
  • Festivals timed to the right moment — Diwali at pradosha, Shivaratri at nishita
  • Regional calendars: Tamil, Kerala, Bengali, Telugu, Jain and Sikh
  • Read it in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు or தமிழ், with optional Devanagari numerals
  • Home-screen widgets and vrat reminders

Pricing

Free to use for the daily panchang. A single lifetime purchase unlocks the full calendar for any month and year, the festival calendar, and every muhurta and kaal. No subscription.
