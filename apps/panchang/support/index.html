Frequently asked questions

Why do the timings look slightly different from my local panchang?

Panchang timings depend on your exact location. Make sure the app is using your current city so sunrise, sunset, and tithi calculations match where you are.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — panchang calculations are done on your device, so the app works without an internet connection.

How is my data handled?

Panchang doesn't collect any personal data. See the privacy policy for details.
