⏱️ FastRing

Fasting, beautifully simple.

FastRing is a clean, focused fasting timer for iPhone. Start a fast, watch your progress ring fill up, and build a consistent fasting habit — without clutter, accounts, or subscription walls getting in your way.

Download on the App Store Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • One-tap fasting timer with a clear progress ring
  • Popular fasting plans (16:8, 18:6, and more) or custom durations
  • Fasting history to keep your streak going
  • All your data stays on your device
