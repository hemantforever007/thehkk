Privacy Policy — FastRing

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This privacy policy describes how FastRing ("the app") handles your information. The short version: FastRing does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data collection

FastRing does not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties — because we never have it in the first place.

Fasting data

Your fasting sessions, plans, and history are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to any server and are never accessible to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app permanently removes this data.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, FastRing uses local notifications generated on your device to remind you about your fasting windows. No notification data is sent to any server.

Children's privacy

FastRing does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Health disclaimer

FastRing is a timer, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any fasting regimen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact me at hemantforever007@gmail.com.
