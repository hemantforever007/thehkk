Features

Cited, not asserted.

16-zone compass
The full sixteen-zone division rather than the usual eight, on a machined parallax dial with true-north correction.
Interference warnings
Guidance when magnetic conditions are poor, instead of a needle that quietly lies to you.
Home Analyzer
Floor plan, centre, north, room placement — with live score tinting as you work through it.
Every claim cited
A scored report with ranked doshas, sourced verdicts, and remedies rendered verbatim from the source text.
Disagreement shown
32-pada entrance analysis, clearly labelled provisional where the classical sources disagree. No false confidence.
Learn and export
A Learn section on the zones, the Brahmasthan and exactly how the app scores — plus PDF export and a shareable diorama render.
