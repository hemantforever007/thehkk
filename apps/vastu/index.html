Vastu

A precision instrument, not a spiritual gadget.

Coming soon to the App Store

Authentic classical analysis, computed entirely on your device. Vastu is a 16-zone compass and a scored home analyzer with sourced verdicts and remedies — no accounts, no network, and no consultations to sell you.

Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • A 16-zone Vastu compass with a machined parallax dial, true-north correction, and honest accuracy states rather than false confidence
  • Interference guidance when magnetic conditions are poor, instead of a needle that quietly lies to you
  • Home Analyzer: floor plan → centre → north → room placement, with live score tinting as you go
  • A scored report with ranked doshas, sourced verdicts, and remedies rendered verbatim from the source text
  • 32-pada entrance analysis, clearly labelled provisional where the classical sources disagree
  • Property Visit Mode: walk a flat, capture rooms in situ with spread checks, and get a verdict in five minutes
  • A Learn section covering the zones, the Brahmasthan, and a plain-English page on exactly how the app scores
  • PDF export and a shareable diorama render of your analysis

Pricing

The compass and the Learn section are free. A one-time lifetime unlock adds the Home Analyzer, Property Visit Mode, and PDF export. No subscription.
