Frequently asked questions

How do I enter my birth details?

Nakshatra asks for your birth date, time, and place when you first open it, and you can edit them any time in Settings. Everything is computed on your phone from those three inputs — nothing is sent anywhere.

I don’t know my exact birth time.

Mark the birth time as unknown and Nakshatra falls back to a sunrise chart — a chart cast for sunrise on your birth date. Everything that depends on the birth time is clearly marked as reduced-confidence, so you always know which readings rest on an assumed time.

How do I restore my purchases?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed into the same Apple ID you bought with. This brings back Nakshatra Plus on a new phone or after a reinstall.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by me. Open Apple’s manage-subscriptions page, or go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the renewal; Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes everything, because all data lives on your device. You can also export or delete your data from inside the app under Settings.

What ayanamsha does Nakshatra use?

Lahiri by default, with Raman and KP available in Settings.

Are these readings predictions?

No. Every reading is a descriptive, classical association — Shadbala, gochar, and dasha described as the classics describe them — offered for reflection. Nakshatra never predicts, and nothing in it is medical, financial, or legal advice.
