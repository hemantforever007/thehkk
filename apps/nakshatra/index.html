Nakshatra

iOS · Kundli and Dasha

Your nine grahas as living gems — real Shadbala, real dasha, computed on your phone.

Nakshatra is a Vedic astrology (Jyotish) app that renders your nine grahas as living gems. Each gem’s size, glow and mood come from real computed strength — your natal Shadbala, today’s transits (gochar), and the running Vimshottari dasha. Readings, not predictions: every line is descriptive and classical, with no AI and no server — everything is computed on-device and verified against Swiss Ephemeris.

Features

Strength you can see, logic you can check.

Living gems
Your nine grahas rendered as gems whose size, glow and mood come from real computed strength — natal Shadbala, today’s transits (gochar), and the running Vimshottari dasha. Nothing is decorative; every glint is a number.
Orb ring & day line
A daily orb ring and day line with real muhurat times — Abhijit, Rahu Kaal — under a panchang header showing today’s tithi, nakshatra and vara.
Shadbala, in full
Complete six-fold Shadbala breakdowns for every graha, so you can see exactly where each gem’s strength comes from.
Vimshottari timeline
The complete dasha timeline — mahadasha → antardasha → pratyantar — with a reading for each period.
Ask
Ask a question and get dated timing windows, with the step-by-step classical logic behind every answer laid out for you to check.
Kundli charts
North-Indian kundli charts — D1 and D9 — downloadable as images to keep or share.

On device

Readings, not predictions.

Every line in Nakshatra is descriptive and classical, never predictive — and none of it is generated by AI. There is no LLM, no server, and no account: everything is computed on your phone and verified against Swiss Ephemeris.
