Frequently asked

Nobody is marked present today. Is that right?

Yes. Everyone is present by default; you only mark the exceptions — absent, half day, or covered by a replacement. Tap a person on Today to change their status.

I marked the wrong day. Can I fix it?

Open the person’s profile and tap any past day in the calendar to choose the right status. Months are locked a week after their salary is paid.

How do advances work?

Log the advance on the Advances tab and pick which month’s salary it comes out of. It appears as a deduction on that Salary Day automatically.

Will my iPad show the same data?

Yes, if both devices use the same Apple ID and iCloud is on. Settings ’ iCloud shows whether sync is active.

What happens when the free trial ends?

The app becomes read-only: everything stays visible and exportable, and you can unlock editing with a monthly, yearly, or one-time lifetime purchase from Settings.

How do I switch to Hindi?

Settings ’ Language, or the switch at the top of the first screen.
