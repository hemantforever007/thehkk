Features

Every date, with the reasoning shown.

Muhurat finder
Auspicious dates for weddings, engagements, griha pravesh, mundan, and namkaran, computed on-device from tithi, nakshatra, bhadra windows, kharmas, and combustion. Every date shows why it works.
Personalized ranking
Add birth details and dates re-rank by tara bala and chandra bala for each person. If a birth time is unknown, Lagna shows both candidate nakshatras rather than guessing.
Lagna countdown
A themed hero screen with a Lock Screen Live Activity, shareable by link. Recipients get the full countdown free — no account, no server, just the link.
Runsheet
Pick your tradition — Punjabi, Marwari, Gujarati, South Indian, and more — toggle off what your family doesn’t do, and drag to reorder. Every time derives from the locked muhurat, which never moves.
Day-of Timekeeper
Giant NOW/NEXT cards and one-thumb +15/+30/Skip buttons that re-flow the whole schedule around the muhurat, and propose what to cut if the day runs short.
Shagun QR studio
Themed UPI QR posters with a mandatory test-scan gate before export. Lagna never touches the money — it only renders an image of your own UPI ID.

On device

Your janma details never leave your phone.

Muhurat calculations run from a built-in panchang astronomy engine, entirely on-device — no network requests, no account. Hindi and English throughout, with Siri Shortcuts and Lock Screen widgets built in.
