GymLoop

iOS · Rotation-based splits

Miss a Tuesday and the plan shouldn’t fall apart.

Most trainers pin workouts to weekdays, so one skipped session throws off the week. GymLoop tracks your position in the rotation instead — Push, Pull, Legs, and back around. Finish a session and the loop advances. Whenever you next walk in, it already knows what’s up.

Features

Built around the loop.

Rotation, not calendar
The app tracks where you are in the cycle, so skipped days never knock your week out of alignment.
A gap detector
Notices when you have been away and offers a full-body reset day, then hands control straight back to the normal rotation.
On your glance screen
A Next Workout home-screen widget, plus Live Activity and Lock Screen tracking for the session you are in.
Trends worth having
A GitHub-style training heatmap with streaks, and a Trends dashboard with e1RM and volume over time.
Health, alongside
Apple Health import shows your body weight and your Watch or Fitness+ sessions next to your logged training.
Fits your routine
Siri Shortcuts, a leave-home reminder based on your gym and travel time, and custom body measurements in kg/cm or lb/in.

On device

Your training log is nobody else’s data set.

No account, no leaderboard, no social feed. Workouts are stored on your device and sync only through your own iCloud. Nothing is uploaded to me, and nothing is measured about you.

Pricing

Free and Pro.

The core of the app is free, and stays free. Free covers the workout loop and rotation, the split editor, your last 30 days of history, the heatmap and streaks, the BMI card, reminders, Siri Shortcuts, home-screen widgets, and iCloud sync.

Pro adds unlimited history, the Trends dashboard, the progress-photo studio, custom body measurements, Apple Health workout import, and Live Activity with Lock Screen widgets.

Three ways to buy Pro

  • Monthly — $2.99 per month
  • Annual — $19.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial
  • Lifetime — $49.99 once, no subscription

Prices shown are US pricing; the App Store converts to your local currency. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled, and you can cancel any time in iOS Settings — the support page has the steps.

Your data is never held hostage. If a subscription lapses, nothing is hidden or deleted — older history simply collapses behind the unlock row and comes straight back if you resubscribe.

Purchases are subject to Apple’s standard EULA.
