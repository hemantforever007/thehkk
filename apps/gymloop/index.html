GymLoop

Your split, not your calendar.

Coming soon to the App Store

GymLoop tracks your position in a rotation — Push, Pull, Legs, and back around — instead of pinning workouts to days of the week. Miss a Tuesday and nothing shifts; the next workout is simply the next one. Offline-first, no account, everything on your device and your own iCloud.

Support Privacy Policy

Features

  • Rotation-based splits: the app tracks where you are in the cycle, so skipped days never knock your week out of alignment
  • A gap detector that notices when you have been away and offers a full-body reset day, then hands control back to the normal rotation
  • Live Activity and Lock Screen tracking for the session you are in, with a Next Workout home-screen widget
  • A GitHub-style training heatmap with streaks, plus e1RM and volume trends
  • Apple Health import: your body weight and your Watch or Fitness+ sessions shown alongside your logged training
  • A guided progress-photo camera with a pose stencil, a ghost overlay of your last shot, and a self-timer
  • Siri Shortcuts — “Start my workout in GymLoop”, “What’s next in GymLoop”
  • A leave-home reminder that fires before you need to depart, based on your gym time and travel time
  • Custom body measurements alongside the built-in ones, in kg/cm or lb/in
